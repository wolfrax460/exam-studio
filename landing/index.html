How to use

  1. Add your notes (paste or upload).
  2. Choose the unit or module.
  3. Generate study notes, handout, exam or flashcards.
  4. Review and edit.
  5. Export to Word/PDF.

Tip: generate one unit at a time for best results.

FAQ

Are my notes stored?

Library items are stored per user. Notes are sent to the AI service you configure.

Can I use my own model?

Yes, plans that allow it let you add your own API key.

Privacy

Notes are sent to the AI service. We store accounts, library items and usage. No note content in logs.
